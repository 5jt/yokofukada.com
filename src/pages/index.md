Yoko Fukada is an artist from Japan.
She is particularly interested in cultural issues and installations relating to the environment, landscape and gardens. She is now temporarily based in Sydney. She is working and researching in Australian history in co-ordination with contemporary artists, and recently curated art exhibitions in Sydney.
