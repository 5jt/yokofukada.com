Yoko Fukada graduated from the University of Art, London (Central Saint Martins), and completed her MA studies at the Architectural Association, U.K.

Having previously worked in art, sculpture, jewelry design and headwear & hat design, Yoko recently shifted her creative focus to Obi. Basing her studio in Tokyo, she works between London and Japan.

<div class="cv" markdown="1">
Yoko Fukada artist & curator
Research fields: Art, Culture and Environmental Issues, Urban Landscape and Gardens.
Education: Master of Art (Histories and Theories Architecture, Urbanism) at the Architectural Association in London.
BA Fine Art at Central St Martins Collage of Art in London.
Previously studied Arts, Sculpture, Paintings, Glass works, Garden Design, Gemmology and Jewellery making.
Yoko established a jewellery design studio in 1985 and worked in NY at Bergdorf Goodman, in London at Hennell in Bond Street and in Tokyo in Ginza Tenshodo.
She is a member of the Royal british Sculpture Association.
